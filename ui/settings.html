MyFlow

Hold a key, speak, and the text appears at your cursor. Changes save automatically.

Speech engine

The WebView's own speech engine. Works offline-ish, understands Hindi, English and Hinglish.
Create a key at console.groq.com/keys. It is stored only in this app's settings file on this PC.
Your own Groq account's models — nothing is pre-filled. Pick whichever one you want to transcribe the clip.

Language

Hinglish keeps Hindi words in Latin script ("mera naam") and English words in English. Hindi returns Devanagari. This applies to the Google engine; Groq detects the language from the audio.

Writing style

With a style on, the raw transcript goes through a Groq model first: grammar, punctuation and filler words are cleaned up, spoken enumerations ("first…, second…") become a tidy list, and the tone is adjusted. Meaning and facts are kept, nothing is added, and the language is not translated.
This box is the exact text sent to the model before your transcript. The style buttons fill it, Customize Prompt empties it so you can write your own. An empty box means plain clean-up.
Uses the same Groq API key. The model reads the transcript, fixes word and grammar mistakes, turns spoken enumerations into a clean list, and rewrites everything in the chosen tone. If the request fails, the raw transcript is pasted instead.

Dictation keys

Smart: hold to talk, release to paste, double-tap to lock recording on or off.
